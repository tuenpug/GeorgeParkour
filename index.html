    How to play

    Controls

    ←↑↓→Move (WASD works too) FHold to run. Tap F twice and hold to sprint (uses energy twice as fast). Running uses the energy bar; walk to recharge SpaceJump, again in the air to flip. Near equipment: the parkour move ZCrouch & crawl, slide when running, perfect roll before landing EGrab ropes, bars, ladders and walls GSpecial move in the air. Choose it in Special moves and unlock more by clearing levels XTrick: 360 spin, high five the coach H BHint, back to the start of the stage M N EscSound, music, pause

    There are 3 levels with 5 stages each (1-1 to 3-5). Every stage is a mini course with 3 or 4 moves in a row. George must finish each move before he can go on. If the clock runs out, he tries that move again. Each move has 30 seconds in Level 1, 25 seconds in Level 2 and 20 seconds in Level 3. Stages start easy and mix more skills as you go. After each stage you can go on to the next one or try again. Free play opens the whole gym, including a giant free-play zone. Drag the screen to look around, scroll to zoom.
